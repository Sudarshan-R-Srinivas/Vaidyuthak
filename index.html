A State Level Techno Cultural Fest

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at The National Institute of Engineering, Mysuru, is excited to announce the return of the ECE Fest, Vaidyuthak 2024! After the success of Vaidyuthak 2023, we're ready to make this year's event even more spectacular.
Join us to showcase your skills and compete in exciting events. Whether you're into engineering projects, coding challenges, or robotics, there's an event for everyone! This is the perfect platform to display your talent, exchange ideas, and push the boundaries of technology. Mark your calendars for the 9th of December and get ready to demonstrate your creativity and technical prowess.
Vaidyuthak 2024 is not just about competitions – it’s about learning, networking, and exploring the latest advancements in engineering. With engaging activities, expert interactions, and a vibrant atmosphere, this year's fest promises to be an unforgettable celebration of innovation and excellence!
